UA Cast Player — Privacy Policy

2026-10-25

Publisher: Dovbnyak28, individual developer. Legal and privacy contact: dovbnyak@gmail.com. UA-Cast plays sources and files you select. This document explains local processing and transfers, not a promise that every third-party SDK is telemetry-free.

Local data and document access

Private app storage contains playlist sources, provider URLs, favourites, preferences, EPG/icon caches and selected-video references/metadata. Android document grants limit access to the files you select; a document provider can be local or cloud-backed. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location permissions and has no app-account registration.

Provider and icon requests

Requests go from your device to the configured playlist/guide providers and explicitly added icon-pack directories. Provider URLs may contain credentials. The app’s channel-icon resolver does not use playlist or EPG logo fields as fallback image sources. Providers receive normal request metadata, including IP address and user-agent, and operate under their own terms.

Casting and phone-to-TV control

When you request casting, your receiver receives the media/temporary relay URL and relevant metadata; a local-file or relayed session also transmits media bytes. Pairing shares session information, displayed media/channel names, playback state and commands between your devices. These local connections may use unencrypted HTTP for receiver compatibility. A session token authorises access but does not encrypt traffic. Use a trusted network and receiver; provider restrictions still apply.

Google SDKs and updates

The developer operates no analytics backend or automatic crash-upload service, and no advertising SDK is integrated. Google Cast can transmit anonymised discovery/session events, SDK/app metadata and device information to Google for SDK improvement; the developer does not control that SDK retention or opt-out. Read https://developers.google.com/cast/docs/android_sender/data_disclosure and https://policies.google.com/privacy. If Google Play checkout is enabled, Google processes purchase information under its policies. GitHub update checks/downloads expose normal network metadata to GitHub, not your playlist or viewing history.

Backup and external copies

New backups exported through Settings are encrypted with your passphrase and can include provider credentials, private links, preferences, favourites and embedded local playlists. Old unencrypted JSON backups can still be imported; keep those private. You choose the document provider and whether files are stored on a phone, computer or in the cloud. There is no developer password-recovery service. Android backup/data-extraction settings exclude private app data; behaviour of vendor tools outside the app is not guaranteed.

Optional support reports

The app creates a diagnostic report locally and opens your chosen sharing application; it does not send the report automatically. The prepared support recipient is dovbnyak@hotmail.com. Reports contain device/app details and activity logs. Recognised secrets are redacted, but provider hosts, LAN addresses, media/channel names or technical identifiers can remain. Review before sending. The sharing provider applies its policies. Reports received by the developer are used for support, retained only as needed for that purpose or legal obligations, then deleted; contact the privacy address to request deletion of an identifiable report.

Retention and deletion

Caches/settings remain locally until replaced, cleared in Settings or removed with app data. Clearing/uninstalling the app does not delete exported backups, copies on other devices, already sent emails or data held independently by providers/SDKs. Delete external copies through their provider. The app has no user accounts to delete.

Children and future changes

The app is not directed at children. Parental-control features do not make it a child-directed service. Future advertising or other data-processing integrations require updated disclosures and any applicable consent before activation. Material changes are published with a new date; enquiries can be sent to the privacy contact.
